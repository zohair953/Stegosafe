Security & Privacy Notice

StegoSafe processes images and messages locally in your browser. Resizing, editing, lossy compression or converting an image to JPEG can destroy hidden data. Steganography hides a message but does not guarantee that the image cannot be analyzed. AES-CBC provides confidentiality but does not independently authenticate ciphertext. Use trusted images and strong passwords.
